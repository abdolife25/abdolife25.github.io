**Voyagelo is a budget travel site that shows what a trip really costs.** Every guide gives prices in US dollars for beds, food, transport and sights, so you can plan a trip and a budget before you book anything.

## What we publish

- **Trip-cost guides** for each country: what a day and a week cost on a tight budget and at mid-range, with a day-by-day route.
- **Where to stay, what to see and when to go**, written for people who want to spend less without missing the best parts.
- **Splurge picks** kept separate, for the days you want to spend more.

## How we check prices

- We check prices against current travel-cost guides, official ticket and fare pages and operator sites, and we say which date they were checked.
- Every guide shows a "Prices checked" date and a "Last updated" date near the top.
- Prices are shown in US dollars. Where a price is set in another currency, we show it and the rate we used.
- Prices change. Where sources disagree or we could not confirm a price, the guide says so instead of guessing.

## How Voyagelo earns money

Some links in our guides are affiliate links. If you book through them, Voyagelo may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This never changes the price you pay, and we only link to services we would suggest to a traveller on a budget. Read the full [affiliate disclosure](/p/affiliate-disclosure.html).

## Contact

Questions, corrections or a price that looks out of date? Use the [contact page](/p/contact.html) and tell us which guide and what you found. We update guides when prices change.
