Questions about a guide, a price that needs updating, or an idea for a destination? Write to us at **[contact@voyagelo.com](mailto:contact@voyagelo.com)**.

**What to write to us about**

- **Corrections and price updates:** if a cost, opening time or booking detail in a guide is out of date, tell us which page and what changed. We update guides when we confirm the facts.
- **Partnerships and travel brands:** if you run a travel product or service and would like us to consider it, send a short description and a link.
- **Everything else:** general questions about the site and our trip tools are welcome too.

We read every message and reply as soon as we can. Please don't send passwords, card numbers or passport details by email.

For how we earn money from the links in our guides, see our [affiliate disclosure](/p/affiliate-disclosure.html). For how we handle your data, see our [privacy policy](/p/privacy-policy.html).
