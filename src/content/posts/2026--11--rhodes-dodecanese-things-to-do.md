**The best things to do in Rhodes are cheap: the medieval Old Town, the Street of the Knights, the moat walk and the Monte Smith sunset are all free, and the two paid headliners, the Palace of the Grand Master and the Acropolis of Lindos, cost about $9 and $13.** A day in Rhodes Town can cost as little as $14 and a Lindos day about $31–42. This guide ranks what is worth paying for, with prices in US dollars. It focuses on Rhodes, the main island of the Dodecanese.

## The best things to do in Rhodes, ranked by value

| Rank | What | Cost | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rhodes Old Town, Street of the Knights and the moat walk | Free | Half a day |
| 2 | Monte Smith sunset | Free | 1–2 hours |
| 3 | Palace of the Grand Master | €8 ($8.80) | 1–2 hours |
| 4 | Acropolis of Lindos and Lindos village | €12 ($13) plus bus | Half a day |
| 5 | Mandraki harbour and Elli beach | Free (sunbeds extra) | 1–2 hours |
| 6 | Archaeological Museum of Rhodes | €6 ($6.60) | 1 hour |

**1. The Old Town.** The car-free lanes of the medieval city cost nothing to walk, and neither do the Street of the Knights or the dry moat around the walls, which one guide calls one of the best walks in town.

**2. Monte Smith.** The hilltop acropolis with the Temple of Apollo is open and free, with a sunset view over the town and the sea.

**3. The Palace of the Grand Master.** The knights' fortress is the main paid sight in Rhodes Town. The Rhodes tourism board listed it at €8 ($8.80) in 2024, open daily 08:00 to 20:00 in summer with last entry 20 minutes before closing. E-tickets are sold on hhticket.gr.

**4. The Acropolis of Lindos.** Lindos is the island's famous village, with a whitewashed old town below an ancient acropolis. The ticket is €12 ($13) in summer and about €6 ($6.60) from November to March, and the bus from Rhodes Town is about €5.50 ($6) each way, taking about 90 minutes. The village streets and the beach below are free, so the paid part is the climb to the top.

**5. Mandraki harbour and Elli beach.** The harbour with its deer statues and medieval windmills is free, and Elli beach is a short walk from the walls. The beach is free, though sunbeds cost extra.

![Bronze deer statues on stone columns at the entrance to Mandraki harbour](/images/rhodes-mandraki-deer-columns.jpg)

**6. The Archaeological Museum.** €6 ($6.60) in 2024. State museums and sites are free on the first Sunday of the month from November to March, which is a good reason to visit in winter.

## What to skip or pay for carefully

- **Taxis to Lindos.** About €60–75 ($66–83) for the full trip, against about €5.50 ($6) on the bus.
- **Car hire in summer.** A car is about €35–50 ($38–55) a day in spring and autumn and above €70 ($77) in July and August. Rhodes Town and Lindos are easy by bus, so hire only for the days you want beaches off the bus routes.
- **Old Town squares.** A beer is €4–6 ($4.40–6.60) in town and more on the Old Town squares. Eat a block back from the main squares.
- **Fish by the kilo.** It is priced by weight, so ask the price before you order.

## Two budget day plans

**A Rhodes Town day, from about $14 to $45.** Walk the Old Town, the Street of the Knights and the moat (free), visit the Palace of the Grand Master (€8, $8.80), optionally the museum (€6, $6.60), eat a souvlaki at under €5 ($5.50), and finish at Monte Smith for sunset (free). That is about $14 with street food only (the Palace ticket plus a souvlaki). A taverna dinner of €15–22 ($17–24) and the museum take it to about $31–45.

**A Lindos day, about $31–42.** Take the KTEL bus both ways (about €11, $12), climb the acropolis (€12, $13) and eat a simple lunch for €5–15 ($5.50–17). Swim at the beach below the village for free.

## Beyond Rhodes: the Dodecanese

The other Dodecanese islands, such as Symi and Kos, are reached by boat from Rhodes. Fares and timetables change by season, so check Ferryhopper for your date before you plan a day trip. This guide covers Rhodes only.

## Cheapest time to go

Spring and autumn are the best balance of weather and price, and car hire is far cheaper outside July and August. Winter is the cheapest time for tickets, with the Lindos ticket at about half price, but hours are shorter.

## Planning Rhodes from your country

For most visitors the flight is the biggest single cost, so check fares from your own airport and compare more than one airline. ## Frequently asked questions

**How much is the Acropolis of Lindos?** About €12 ($13) in summer and about €6 ($6.60) from November to March, plus about €5.50 ($6) each way on the bus from Rhodes Town.

**How much is the Palace of the Grand Master?** The Rhodes tourism board listed it at €8 ($8.80) in 2024, so confirm the current price on hhticket.gr.

**What is free in Rhodes?** The Old Town lanes, the Street of the Knights, the moat walk, Monte Smith, Mandraki harbour and Elli beach.

**Do I need a car in Rhodes?** Not for Rhodes Town and Lindos, which the buses reach. A car at about €35–50 ($38–55) a day in spring and autumn helps for beaches off the bus routes.

**How do I get from the airport to Rhodes Town?** The airport bus is about €2.60 ($2.85), and the airport is about 25 minutes from town by taxi.
