## Check the situation before you book

Dubai's tourism was hit hard in 2026. A regional conflict involving Iran escalated in late February, which caused flight cancellations and airspace closures across the Gulf, and hotel occupancy fell to 36% in March before recovering to 66% in August, according to Dubai's tourism department. Airport capacity had recovered to about 84% of its pre-conflict level by September, but advice from some governments still warns of possible cancellations and periodic airspace closures (the UK's advice was last updated on 12 October). Check your own government's travel advice before you book, choose refundable or flexible fares and rooms where you can, and make sure your travel insurance covers disruption. The upside for budget travelers: hotel rates this winter are forecast to be about 10–15% below 2025.

## What Dubai costs per day (2027)

| | Tight budget | Mid-range |
|---|---|---|
| Bed | Hostel dorm $20–40 | 3–4 star hotel $85–150 |
| Food | $15–25 | $40–70 |
| Local transport | $5–10 | $11–19 |
| Sights | $8–14 | $17–29 |
| **Per day** | **about $48–85** | **about $140–245** |

Cafeteria plates and shawarma in Karama, Satwa and Deira cost about $4–8, mall food courts about $9–13 and a sit-down restaurant meal about $20–35. A metro ride costs 3–7.5 AED (about $0.80–2), and a shared creek *abra* boat costs 1 AED (about $0.27). A hotel bill normally adds a per-room "tourism dirham" of about 7–20 AED a night, but Dubai exempted it in May 2026, so check what your booking says. These ranges come from several current travel-cost guides, which disagree by a few dollars, so treat them as planning numbers, not quotes.

## What 7 days costs in Dubai

Seven days and six nights, flying in and out of Dubai International (DXB).

| Cost (6 nights, 7 days) | Tight budget | Mid-range |
|---|---|---|
| Beds | $140–250 | $510–900 |
| Food | $105–175 | $280–490 |
| Local transport (including airport metro) | $35–70 | $75–135 |
| Sights and tours | $55–100 | $120–200 |
| **Total** | **about $335–595** | **about $985–1,725** |

A tight budget here means dorm beds and cafeteria food. If you want a private room, expect to land nearer the $480–700 range that other guides give for budget travelers. Add 10–15% for extras.

### The route, day by day

#### Days 1–2: Old Dubai

Start in Deira and Bur Dubai, the cheapest parts of the city. Walk through the Al Fahidi Historic District (free), cross Dubai Creek on an *abra* for 1 AED, and browse the Gold and Spice Souks. Eat dinner at a Karama or Satwa cafeteria.

#### Days 3–4: Downtown and the big views

The Burj Khalifa observation deck (At the Top) costs from about 169 AED off-peak (about $46) and 240+ AED (about $65+) for sunset slots, so book a daytime slot. Dubai Mall is free to enter, the Dubai Fountain show is free to watch from the promenade after 6 pm, and the Dubai Frame is about $14. The Museum of the Future is 169 AED (about $46).

#### Days 5–6: Beach, marina and desert

Public beaches such as Kite Beach and JBR are free, and the Marina and JBR promenades cost nothing to walk. A shared evening desert safari starts from about 150 AED (about $41), which is the biggest single treat on a tight budget.

The metro runs to the airport, which is far cheaper than a taxi.

## Cheapest time to go

The cheapest months are May to September, when hotel and flight prices can be 40–60% lower, but highs reach 40–45°C. April and October offer the best balance of price and weather. November to March is peak season, with the most comfortable weather and the highest prices; Ramadan brings hotel promotions but restricts eating in public during daylight.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much money do I need for 7 days in Dubai?** About $335–595 for a tight budget with dorm beds and $985–1,725 at mid-range, excluding flights, based on the route above.

**Is Dubai expensive?** It can be, but the expensive parts are hotels in peak season, alcohol (about 45–70 AED a drink) and big attractions. Food at local cafeterias, public transport and the free sights keep a day cheap.

**Is Dubai cheaper in summer?** Yes, by 40–60% on hotels and flights in May to September, but outdoor sightseeing is hard in 40–45°C heat, so plan indoor attractions and evenings.

**Do I need a metro card?** Yes. A nol card costs 25 AED and includes 19 AED of credit, and metro fares are 3 AED for one zone and 5 AED for two.
