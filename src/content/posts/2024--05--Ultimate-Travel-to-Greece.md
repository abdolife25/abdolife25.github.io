Greece, a country steeped in history and natural beauty, beckons travelers with its stunning landscapes, crystal-clear waters, and rich cultural heritage. Whether you're drawn by its ancient ruins, idyllic beaches, or vibrant cities, this guide will help you plan the perfect week in Greece, focusing on attractions, beaches, and key tourist spots. We’ll also break down a total budget for your stay, including accommodations, activities, and dining options.

## Best Time to Visit Greece

When planning your trip, timing is crucial. The best time to visit Greece is during the spring (April to June) and fall (September to October). During these months, you can enjoy pleasant weather, fewer crowds, and lower prices. The summer months (July and August) are peak tourist season, attracting large crowds, especially on popular islands and beaches.

## Attractions in Greece

### Historical Attractions

1. **Acropolis of Athens**

   ![Historical Attractions](/images/unnamed.webp)
   - **Cost**: €20 per person
   - **Benefits**: UNESCO World Heritage site, stunning views of Athens.
   - **Features**: Parthenon, Erechtheion, and Temple of Athena Nike.
   - **More Information**: [Visit Greece](https://www.visitgreece.gr)
2. **Delphi Archaeological Site**

   ![Historical Attractions](/images/ancient-site-top-1-1280.webp)
   - **Cost**: €12 per person
   - **Benefits**: Historical significance, beautiful mountainous scenery.
   - **Features**: Temple of Apollo, ancient theater, and museum.
   - **More Information**: [Visit Greece](https://www.visitgreece.gr)

### Natural Attractions

1. **Meteora**

   ![Natural Attractions](/images/5.webp)
   - **Cost**: Free to explore; monastery entry €3
   - **Benefits**: Unique rock formations, UNESCO World Heritage site.
   - **Features**: Six monasteries perched atop cliffs.
   - **More Information**: [Meteora](https://www.visitmeteora.com)
2. **Samaria Gorge (Crete)**

   ![Natural Attractions](/images/samaria-gorge-crete_feature-scaled.webp)
   - **Cost**: €5 per person
   - **Benefits**: Breathtaking landscapes, hiking opportunities.
   - **Features**: 16 km hike through one of Europe’s longest gorges.
   - **More Information**: [Samaria Gorge](https://www.samariagorge.gr)

## Beaches in Greece

### Best Beaches in Greece

1. **Navagio Beach (Zakynthos)**

   ![Best Beaches in Greece](/images/19.webp)
   - **Cost**: Accessible by boat; boat trips from €20.
   - **Benefits**: Iconic shipwreck cove, stunning turquoise waters.
   - **Features**: Photogenic landscapes and surrounding cliffs.
   - **More Information**: [Visit Zakynthos](https://www.visit-zakynthos.com)
2. **Elafonissi Beach (Crete)**

   ![Best Beaches in Greece](/images/elafonisi-crete-scaled.webp)
   - **Cost**: Free entry.
   - **Benefits**: Pink sand, shallow waters, ideal for families.
   - **Features**: Natural reserve, great for swimming and sunbathing.
   - **More Information**: [Elafonissi Beach](https://www.cretanbeaches.com)
3. **Mykonos Beaches (e.g., Psarou, Paradise)**

   ![Best Beaches in Greece](/images/mykonos-beach-psarrou.webp)
   - **Cost**: Free entry; sunbeds and umbrellas around €20.
   - **Benefits**: Vibrant beach clubs, lively atmosphere.
   - **Features**: Nightlife and dining options nearby.
   - **More Information**: [Mykonos Beach Guide](https://www.mykonos.gr)
4. **Balos Lagoon (Crete)**

   ![Best Beaches in Greece](/images/img_3206-1920x1279.webp)
   - **Cost**: Boat trip from €25, or hike in for free.
   - **Benefits**: Stunning natural beauty, ideal for photography.
   - **Features**: Shallow waters and picturesque surroundings.
   - **More Information**: [Balos Lagoon](https://www.baloslagoon.com)

## Cruise in Greece

Exploring Greece by cruise is a fantastic way to see multiple islands in a short time. Various companies offer day cruises or multi-day packages that include stops at popular islands like Mykonos, Santorini, and Crete.
