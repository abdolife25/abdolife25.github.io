Cambodia is an enchanting destination that offers travelers a rich cultural history, stunning natural landscapes, and vibrant city life, all at an affordable price. From the majestic Angkor Wat to the bustling streets of Phnom Penh, Cambodia caters to both budget and luxury travelers alike. Whether you’re planning a holiday in Cambodia or embarking on a budget travel adventure, this guide will help you explore the country's best attractions, the ideal time to visit, and tips for making the most of your trip without breaking the bank.

### Best Time to Visit Cambodia

Cambodia enjoys a tropical climate, which can be divided into two main seasons: the wet season (May to October) and the dry season (November to April). The best time to visit Cambodia is during the cool, dry season from November to February. This period offers the most pleasant weather, with average temperatures ranging between 25°C to 30°C (77°F to 86°F), making it perfect for sightseeing and outdoor activities.

However, if you're looking to save money, traveling during the wet season has its advantages. Fewer tourists mean lower accommodation prices, and while you may experience afternoon showers, the lush landscapes and smaller crowds at major attractions make it worthwhile.

### Top Attractions in Cambodia

Cambodia is a treasure trove of history, culture, and natural beauty. Here are the must-visit attractions in Cambodia:

#### 1. **Angkor Wat**

![1. Angkor Wat](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/a/AVvXsEjmKJs13XCJf-0KK-fSIuh7W8jlLHN3d2YGjDW-iXFOel5dORZ95jAx25fImFoopMdw68kJrGqEbUswO7bA8VRch-kILjIyMHVCM9BgD-y9NigO8Bnip6uPsIq_Iw-kM8ZGqR5O1dlXeWzPZLe-F4_6G787Y7-zEzjJmbf97F91ZfwCrrRTRMvCdg7-=w581-h388)

The world-renowned Angkor Wat is the largest religious monument globally and a UNESCO World Heritage site. Visiting this iconic temple complex is a must on any Cambodian itinerary. The best time to visit Angkor Wat is during sunrise when the temple is bathed in golden light.

#### 2. **Phnom Penh**

![2. Phnom Penh](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/a/AVvXsEi0wyP0OiCXgfDGTtk8xsTjzR5rTOzYnbolsANpxoYeSZcJaU3bmTuR2tUw8bdO2oABot6m045ZdtZHVjlc48k6J2FtQ42PayzzdJi_szi92ahrmdqmcu2aVHnXE7ztRp4pypxzaldJrRrVeWi0meMfJ29etYmZLiNd_VFuSLupvwhFKNe5wwmiuhPI=w596-h283)

The capital city of Cambodia, Phnom Penh, is home to the Royal Palace, the Silver Pagoda, and the somber but important Tuol Sleng Genocide Museum. It’s a vibrant city offering a blend of rich history and modern life.

#### 3. **Siem Reap**

![3. Siem Reap](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/a/AVvXsEgoxpMrczDYYgV6z9v-XMRdPVE-FwFBch3eus9ohrReMu_A1ELT88nhZZLhATIVd_5BAW2Ujm0xq5jl-_PjXdiD2E34R8rMTfbCf-tYG4ImLJffmOLvt5dg5es9M-U3D59gk85fwdLodHlOPDWWu4L3a_o6lleKJTIqV854yuM4ls5tbTFFSfjqFpp8=w573-h384)

Known as the gateway to Angkor Wat, Siem Reap is a bustling town with lively markets, trendy cafés, and a fantastic nightlife scene. It’s the perfect base for exploring the Angkor temple complex.

For beach lovers, Koh Rong is a paradise island with pristine white sands and crystal-clear waters. It’s a great spot for snorkeling, diving, and simply relaxing by the sea.

This sleepy riverside town is famous for its French colonial architecture, beautiful temples, and the iconic bamboo train. It’s the perfect destination for those looking to experience Cambodia's countryside charm.

### Budget Travel in Cambodia

Cambodia is an excellent destination for budget travelers. With affordable accommodations, cheap street food, and low-cost transportation, you can easily explore the country without overspending.

#### **Flights**

Depending on your starting location, flights to Cambodia can range from $300 to $700, with the most budget-friendly options available during the wet season. Phnom Penh International Airport and Siem Reap International Airport are the major gateways to the country.

#### **Accommodation**

Budget travelers can find hostels and guesthouses starting from as low as $5 per night. Mid-range hotels offer comfortable stays for around $20-$30 per night. For those seeking luxury, Cambodia has a range of upscale hotels and resorts available for $100 and above.

#### **Food**

Cambodian street food is both delicious and affordable. A meal at a local market costs around $2 to $3, while dining at a mid-range restaurant will set you back about $10. Popular dishes include *amok* (a traditional coconut milk curry) and *nom banh chok* (Khmer noodles).

#### **Transportation**

Getting around Cambodia is easy and affordable. Tuk-tuks are a popular and cheap option for short trips, costing about $1-$2 for a ride. Buses and minibusses are available for longer journeys between cities, with tickets ranging from $5 to $10 depending on the distance. For budget travelers, renting a bicycle or a motorbike is also a great way to explore cities and rural areas.

#### **Total Daily Budget**

On average, a budget traveler can expect to spend around $25-$30 per day in Cambodia, which includes accommodation, food, and transportation.

### Best Time to Visit Cambodia for Budget Travel

The best time to visit Cambodia for budget travelers is during the wet season (May to October). While the weather is more humid, this is the low tourist season, meaning you'll find cheaper accommodation, discounted tours, and fewer crowds at major attractions. Additionally, the rainy season brings out Cambodia’s lush, green landscapes, making it a beautiful time to explore the countryside.

For those looking to avoid the rain but still save on costs, consider visiting in the shoulder months of May and October when the weather is transitioning but prices remain lower than during the peak season.

### Holiday Tips for Cambodia

#### **Cultural Etiquette**

Cambodia is a country steeped in tradition, and it's essential to respect the local customs. Always dress modestly when visiting temples, covering your shoulders and knees. When greeting locals, it's customary to place your hands together in a prayer-like position and offer a slight bow, known as the *Sampeah*.

#### **Safety and Health**

While Cambodia is generally a safe destination, it’s important to take precautions such as avoiding unlicensed taxis and being cautious with your belongings, especially in crowded areas. Health-wise, be sure to drink bottled water and avoid consuming ice from street vendors.

#### **Visa Requirements**

Most travelers will need a visa to enter Cambodia, which can be obtained on arrival at the airport or applied for online through the e-visa system. A tourist visa typically costs around $30 and is valid for 30 days. Be sure to check the visa requirements for your specific country before traveling.

### Final Total Budget for a Week in Cambodia

For budget-conscious travelers, a one-week trip to Cambodia can be incredibly affordable. Here's a breakdown of the total average budget for a week:

- **Flights:** $400 (depending on location)
- **Accommodation:** $5-$10 per night ($35-$70 for a week)
- **Food:** $10 per day ($70 for a week)
- **Transportation:** $3-$5 per day ($21-$35 for a week)
- **Attractions and Activities:** $50 (including Angkor Wat entry fee)

**Total Estimated Budget for a Week:** $576 - $625

Cambodia is one of Southeast Asia’s most affordable destinations, offering travelers a chance to explore incredible history, culture, and natural beauty without breaking the bank. Whether you’re visiting the temples of Angkor or relaxing on the beaches of Koh Rong, Cambodia is a budget traveler's dream.
