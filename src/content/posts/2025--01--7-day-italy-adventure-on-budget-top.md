Italy offers a rich tapestry of history, culture, art, and cuisine that makes it one of the best travel destinations in the world. Whether you're keen on exploring **tourist spots in Italy**, savoring its world-famous cuisine, or seeking **budget travel in Italy**, this 7-day itinerary has you covered, with all the details you'll need: from places to visit, food to try, and tips on affordable accommodations, transportation, and must-see tourist spots.

### **Total Estimated Cost of a 7-Day Budget Trip to Italy**

After exploring all that Italy has to offer, here's a breakdown of the estimated cost for a budget-conscious traveler:

1. **Accommodation (Cheap Hotels in Italy)**:

   - Average cost per night in budget hotels or hostels: **$50 - $100**.
   - Total for 7 nights: **$350 - $700**.
2. **Food (Affordable Meals & Street Food)**:

   - Breakfast: **$5 - $10** per day.
   - Lunch: **$10 - $20** per day.
   - Dinner: **$15 - $30** per day.
   - Total food cost for 7 days: **$210 - $420**.
3. **Transportation (Trains, Buses, Metro)**:

   - Local transportation: **$5 - $10** per day.
   - Total for 7 days: **$35 - $70**.
   - Train tickets for intercity travel (Florence to Rome, for example): **$30 - $50** per trip.
   - Total train costs for 2-3 trips: **$60 - $150**.
4. **Attractions & Activities**:

   - Entry fees for popular attractions (Colosseum, museums, etc.): **$10 - $20** per attraction.
   - Total for 4-5 attractions: **$40 - $100**.
5. **Miscellaneous (Souvenirs, Shopping)**:

   - Average spend on shopping and souvenirs: **$30 - $50**.

### **Estimated Total Cost for 7 Days in Italy**:

- **Low Budget Estimate**: **$725**
- **Higher-End Budget Estimate**: **$1,420**

#### **Day 1: Arrival in Rome – The Eternal City**

![Day 1: Arrival in Rome – The Eternal City](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/a/AVvXsEjHryqUpjVyuHWJ9UTTqUkvW3Un6PAnogWtledo9nc6A7rzUjqKjyuuE7WQSfB6e0oV5CItDb-FlRSOmEeBl9LhSW8ws5-D2DIRwPxM-GPNPgd_iMXsP2LvxEUAuXSpy5KV2p-k-TnwHvfelUmAur6lRgjNy0EPAkHdozoROS6SU_tIQmR245rbzCXl=w637-h358)

**Accommodation**:

- For a budget-friendly stay, consider **[Hotel Artemide](https://www.hotelartemide.it/)** in Rome. It's centrally located, offers great amenities, and costs around **€90 per night**. Alternatively, if you prefer something even more affordable, **Airbnb** options in Rome can start around **€60 per night**.

**Activities & Tourist Spots**:

1. **[The Colosseum](https://colosseo.it/en/area/the-colosseum/)**: Begin your Roman adventure with a visit to the iconic Colosseum, one of the most famous **tourist spots in Italy**. Entrance fee: **€16**.
2. **Roman Forum & Palatine Hill**: These adjacent sites are rich in ancient history. Entrance fee (combined ticket with Colosseum): **€16**.
3. **[Trevi Fountain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trevi_Fountain)**: Don’t miss the chance to throw a coin into the Trevi Fountain—ensuring your return to Rome. Free entry.
4. **Pantheon**: This ancient building is an architectural marvel and free to enter.
5. **Piazza Navona**: A beautiful square filled with fountains, cafes, and artists. Free entry.

**Food**:

- **Breakfast**: A pastry and espresso at a local café – **€5**.
- **Lunch**: Traditional Roman pasta like *cacio e pepe* or *carbonara* – **€15**.
- **Dinner**: Enjoy a Margherita pizza at a local pizzeria – **€10**.

**Transportation**:

- **Rome Metro Day Pass**: **€7** for unlimited rides on the metro and buses for 24 hours.

#### **Day 2: Rome – Vatican City**

![Day 2: Rome – Vatican City](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/a/AVvXsEj0hiYhXDqgnbLFgq3TuF9Ex0RFEbych5rfr9kF2IcpLulPXuu6WyG_AN4GsFv39Xy1EcdSM6vtgLC0y8kMPS8Abbs7Xx7hLcEwEGnRxTFD-lpYc6vq3naUj7Vurl1WPBlcde1BwgBErQnMoHjN7MY5wLsB-APhMkpVt8aTeAXF9h3lLT0JG_qoGdLd=w592-h394)

**Accommodation**:

- Stay at **Hotel Artemide** or opt for a budget **Airbnb** for about **€60 per night**.

**Activities & Tourist Spots**:

1. **[Vatican Museums](https://www.museivaticani.va/) & Sistine Chapel**: Start the day with a visit to the Vatican Museums, home to some of the world’s most famous artworks, including the Sistine Chapel. Entrance fee: **€17**.
2. **[St. Peter’s Basilica](https://www.basilicasanpietro.va/en/)**: This magnificent church is free to enter, but if you want to climb to the dome for incredible views, the fee is **€10**.
3. **[Castel Sant'Angelo](https://castelsantangelorome.com/)**: Explore this historic castle on the banks of the Tiber River. Entrance fee: **€15**.

**Food**:

- **Breakfast**: A cappuccino and cornetto at a local café – **€5**.
- **Lunch**: Try the classic Roman dish *saltimbocca alla romana* – **€15**.
- **Dinner**: A plate of *cacio e pepe* at a traditional trattoria – **€12**.

**Transportation**:

- **Vatican Museums Skip-the-Line Ticket**: **€22** (pre-book for convenience and to avoid long queues).

#### **Day 3: Florence – Art, Culture, and Food**

![Day 3: Florence – Art, Culture, and Food](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/a/AVvXsEjMS0V1G6-MgAJSYwu1AWRrbF-hTYHNU1lhPTquhpACgoruoFO0p8RnBHuWN8_GMhD-w-vqTym1CBF_dxGXm5JFnSGychs2VbfURYec7vbbindaQQ646Ho5xnTugUP02SnoCKsbSJkxm4ZuvQPu7UYCQQHHybBG7GceSrQ_uuG8c7is1NzFn4am7a3a=w595-h307)

**Accommodation**:

- **Hotel Fiorita** offers budget-friendly stays at around **€70 per night**. Alternatively, try staying in a local **Airbnb** which can be as low as **€50 per night**.

**Activities & Tourist Spots**:

1. **Uffizi Gallery**: Florence is renowned for its art, and the Uffizi Gallery showcases masterpieces from artists like Botticelli and Michelangelo. Entrance fee: **€20**.
2. **Piazza del Duomo & Florence Cathedral**: Visit the stunning Florence Cathedral and its surrounding square. Entrance fee: **€18** for access to the cathedral, dome, and bell tower.
3. **Ponte Vecchio**: Explore this famous medieval bridge lined with jewelry shops. Free entry.
