## Beaches Romania

Romania’s Black Sea coastline is dotted with a range of beautiful beaches that cater to all kinds of travelers. From vibrant party hubs to serene, uncrowded sands, there’s a beach for every type of visitor.

### **Mamaia Beach**

![Mamaia Beach](/images/mamaia-8.webp)

Known as Romania's top beach destination, **Mamaia Beach** is famous for its nightlife, water sports, and soft sandy shorelines.

- **Benefits**: Easy access to restaurants, bars, and affordable accommodations.
- **Cost to Visit**: Free access, but amenities like lounge chairs can be rented for $5 - $10.
- **Unique Feature**: Long stretches of golden sand and lively nightlife.
- **[More information here](https://www.mamaia-beach.ro)**

###

### **Vama Veche**

![Vama Veche](/images/vama-veche-rezervatie.webp)

For those seeking a more alternative, laid-back atmosphere, **Vama Veche** is a bohemian beach near the Bulgarian border.

- **Benefits**: Great for camping, affordable local food and drinks.
- **Cost to Visit**: Free access, with budget accommodations starting from $15 per night.
- **Unique Feature**: Famous for its free-spirited vibe and music festivals.
- **[Find out more here](https://www.vamaveche.ro)**

###

### **Eforie Nord Beach**

![Eforie Nord Beach](/images/the-beach-of-eforie-nord.webp)

A quieter alternative to Mamaia, **Eforie Nord** is popular with families looking for a more relaxed beach vacation.

- **Benefits**: Family-friendly, thermal baths, affordable food options.
- **Cost to Visit**: Free access; accommodation in nearby guesthouses starts at $20 per night.
- **Unique Feature**: Famous for therapeutic mud treatments.
- **[More details here](https://www.eforienord.ro)**

###

### **Costinești Beach**

![Costinești Beach](/images/costinesti-beach.webp)

Costinești is known as a student beach, offering a lively, budget-friendly environment with lots of events and beach parties.

- **Benefits**: Affordable hotels, fun and energetic atmosphere.
- **Cost to Visit**: Free access, nearby hostels starting from $10 per night.
- **Unique Feature**: Shipwreck visible from the shore.
- **[Explore more here](https://www.costinestibeach.ro)**

###

### **Gura Portiței Beach**

![Gura Portiței Beach](https://blogger.googleusercontent.com/img/b/R29vZ2xl/AVvXsEhf6x8PHHP0EvTFv5-DmphcaFfPzWqbDJJLluspqxqMvb20YlYIAcVlNaU7dqrvA_bvR6FhlJ5Vwcw30dTjtkmjEjOeJqTjibADiVM2ugYKjH_WIBmzjQ-zkCQRkIYSpR9ErxKYIr8DkoiMUWoHjU2Zh1cOW997Bjf1aPzaH-KOM1FJhTLrIdZrii7B/w547-h274/AF1QipMp3GST3_4GkUVeoN27DA7EUh4aimGuCZV_pkfc=s1600-k-no.webp)

If you're looking for a more secluded, off-the-beaten-path beach, **Gura Portiței** is your perfect getaway.

## Best Time to Visit Romania

When planning your trip to Romania, timing is key for both budget and weather. The best time to visit Romania for budget travelers is either in the **spring (April - June)** or **fall (September - October)**.

- **Benefits of Spring/Fall**: Fewer crowds, lower prices, pleasant weather.
- **Cost**: Flights and accommodations are generally 20% - 30% cheaper than during the summer months.
- **Unique Feature**: Romania’s natural beauty is on full display, with blooming flowers in the spring and stunning foliage in the fall.

For more on when to visit, check [Romania Travel Tips](https://www.romaniatourism.com).
