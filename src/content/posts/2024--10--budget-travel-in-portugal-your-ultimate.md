Portugal is a fantastic destination for budget travelers, offering a mix of stunning landscapes, rich history, and vibrant culture. In this guide, we’ll explore how to travel Portugal on a budget, covering everything from the best beaches and camping spots to affordable hotels, car rentals, and cheap flights.

**Best Time to Visit Portugal**

The best time to visit Portugal is during the shoulder seasons—spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November). These times offer pleasant weather, fewer tourists, and lower prices for accommodation and flights. **Camping in Portugal**

![Budget Travel in Portugal: Your Ultimate Guide](/images/ericeira-camping-1000x600-1.webp)

Camping is a great way to save money while exploring Portugal. There are several campsites near beaches and national parks that are ideal for budget travelers:

- **Camping Orbitur Guincho, Cascais:** Located near Guincho Beach, perfect for surfing enthusiasts and nature lovers.
- **Camping Orbitur Valado, Nazaré:** A budget-friendly campsite near the famous Nazaré surfing beach.
- **Camping Lima Escape, Peneda-Gerês National Park:** For those who want to explore Portugal’s mountains and forests, this campsite offers affordable accommodation surrounded by nature.
