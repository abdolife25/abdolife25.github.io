**India costs roughly $25–55 a day on a tight budget and $70–130 a day at mid-range, flights and visa not included.** Food, local transport and trains are very cheap. The biggest costs are your flight, your bed and the foreigner prices at the big monuments, where the Taj Mahal alone is about $14. The trick is to eat where locals eat, take the train instead of a private car, and pick which paid sights are worth it.

*Prices checked 19 November 2026 against current travel-cost guides, ticket price lists and rail fare pages. All prices are in US dollars, converted at about ₹94 = $1 (the rupee traded between about 93.6 and 94.1 to the dollar on 16 and 17 November, so rupee prices can shift in dollar terms). Monument tickets are set in rupees.*

## What India costs per day (2027)

| | Tight budget | Mid-range |
|---|---|---|
| Bed | Hostel dorm or budget room $10–25 | Share a 3–4 star double $25–50 each |
| Food | $6–12 | $20–40 |
| Local transport and trains (averaged) | $5–11 | $13–24 |
| Sights (weekly average) | $6–9 | $14–20 |
| **Per day** | **about $25–55** | **about $70–130** |

Local food is where India is cheapest. Recent Delhi price lists show chole bhature (a chickpea curry with fried bread) at about ₹155 ($1.65), a canteen thali (a plate of several dishes with rice and bread) at about ₹259 ($2.75) and a street snack at about ₹111 ($1.20). A mid-range three-course dinner for two is about ₹2,328 ($25), so about $12 each.

Getting around Delhi is cheap too. Metro fares since 25 August 2025 run from ₹11 to ₹64 ($0.12–0.68) depending on distance, and a prepaid taxi starts at about ₹25 ($0.27) for the first 2 km, then about ₹14 ($0.15) per km without air-conditioning. For auto-rickshaws, use the Uber or Ola app so you do not pay inflated tourist prices.

Dorm and hotel prices vary a lot by city and season. One Delhi guide lists a hostel dorm bed at about $22 and a budget hotel at about $23 (typical $18–29), while other guides put dorms at $5–10 elsewhere, so treat the bed row as a planning range, not a quote. Most nationalities also need an e-visa, which is $25 for 30 days from July to March and $10 for April to June, plus a 3% card fee. Check your passport and the official portal, indianvisaonline.gov.in, before you book.

## What 7 days costs in India

Seven days and six nights on the Golden Triangle: two nights in Delhi, two in Agra and two in Jaipur, travelling between them by train.

| Cost (6 nights, 7 days) | Tight budget | Mid-range |
|---|---|---|
| Beds (6 nights) | $60–150 | $150–300 |
| Food | $42–84 | $140–280 |
| Trains and local transport | $36–78 | $93–165 |
| Sights and guides | $45–61 | $100–140 |
| **Total** | **about $185–375** | **about $485–885** |

The low end uses dorms or budget rooms, Standard chair-car trains and the sights you most want. The high end adds all ten sights in the table below and nicer rooms. Mid-range uses executive trains and a mix of taxis, and the guide fees in the mid-range row are rough estimates, because guide prices vary by person. Add 10–15% for extras, and the visa is on top.

The Taj Mahal is ₹1,100 for entry plus ₹200 for the main mausoleum chamber, and the price has stood at ₹1,100 since 2018. One tour site lists ₹1,500 for 2026, and a news report from earlier this year described a proposed increase, so check the official ASI site before you go. The Taj is closed every Friday, so plan your Agra days around that.

### The route, day by day

#### Days 1–2: Delhi

Land in Delhi and ride the Airport Express metro into the city, which costs well under $1. Spend the first day at Humayun's Tomb (about $6) and the Red Fort (about $10), and add Qutub Minar (about $6) on day 2 if you have time. Eat chole bhature and thali for lunch and save your money for the sights.

#### Days 3–4: Agra and the Taj Mahal

Take the Gatimaan Express from Delhi to Agra, a trip of about 1 hour 40 minutes. A recent listing shows the AC chair car at ₹765 ($8) and the executive class at ₹1,495 ($16). The train appears not to run on Fridays, so confirm the running days before you book. Visit Agra Fort (about $7) the first afternoon and go to the Taj Mahal at opening time the next day (about $14), when it is cooler and quieter.

#### Days 5–6: Jaipur

Take a train from Agra to Jaipur. Prices run about $5–21 depending on the train and class, and the fastest trains take about 3 hours 40 minutes. In Jaipur, spend one day at Amer Fort (about $5) and Hawa Mahal (about $2), and the next at the City Palace (about $7) and Jantar Mantar (about $2). The markets and street food are free to browse and cheap to eat from.

#### Day 7: back to Delhi

Take the Vande Bharat to Delhi, which is the fastest train and takes about 3 hours 40 minutes to 4 hours, with chair-car fares from about ₹1,060 ($11). Cheaper trains such as the double-decker run about ₹490–515 ($5) in chair car. Leave plenty of time before your flight.

**How foreigners book trains:** the easiest route is an online agent such as 12Go, which sells confirmed seats and accepts overseas cards. IRCTC works but takes time to register, and Tatkal tickets are effectively closed to foreigners since July 2025. For a train that is full, the International Tourist Bureau at New Delhi station sells tourist-quota seats.

## Beyond the Golden Triangle

This route is the cheapest first trip, but India is huge. If you have more time, popular add-ons include Goa and the Kerala coast for beaches and backwaters, and Varanasi for the Ganges ghats. Each is a flight or a long train away, so price the extra transport before you add them. Our [India guides](/tags/india.html) cover more.

## Cheapest time to go

North India is at its best from about November to February, when days are mild and dry, which is also when many visitors come. April to June is very hot, and July to September is the monsoon. Visa fees are lower from April to June ($10 instead of $25), but the heat makes sightseeing hard work, so most budget travelers weigh that against the saving.

## Planning India from your country

For most visitors the flight is the biggest single cost, so check fares from your own airport and compare more than one airline. Visa and entry rules depend on your passport, so check the official Indian e-visa portal and your own government's current travel advice before you book. ## Frequently asked questions

**How much money do I need for 7 days in India?** About $185–375 for a tight budget and $485–885 at mid-range, excluding flights and visa, based on the Delhi, Agra and Jaipur route above.

**Is India cheap to visit?** Daily costs are low, especially food, local transport and trains. Foreigner ticket prices at the major monuments are higher than what locals pay, but the Taj Mahal is still only about $14.

**How much is the Taj Mahal ticket for foreigners?** About ₹1,300 ($14) including the mausoleum, on most recent price lists. It is closed on Fridays.

**How much is the train from Delhi to Agra?** About ₹765 ($8) in an air-conditioned chair car on the Gatimaan Express, and about ₹1,495 ($16) in executive class. The trip takes about 1 hour 40 minutes.

**Do I need a visa for India?** Most nationalities need an e-visa, which is $25 for 30 days from July to March and $10 from April to June. Check the official portal for your passport.
