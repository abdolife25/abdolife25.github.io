## Why Visit Constanța?

Constanța, Romania’s oldest city, sits on the shores of the Black Sea and is a vibrant mix of history, culture, and stunning coastal landscapes. Known for its golden beaches, charming architecture, and lively atmosphere, Constanța is a must-visit destination. This guide will outline everything you need to know for an unforgettable trip, from where to stay to the best beaches, local food, and hidden gems—all while keeping your budget in check.

## Beaches in Constanța, Romania

### 1. Mamaia Beach1. Mamaia Beach

- **Highlights**: Known as the "Pearl of the Black Sea," Mamaia Beach is famous for its soft golden sand and clear waters.
- **Cost**: Free entry; $5–$10 for sunbed rentals.
- **Why Visit?** Perfect for water sports, beach bars, and family-friendly activities.
- [Plan your trip here](/2024/10/budget-travel-guide-to-romania-explore.html).

### 2. Modern Beach2. Modern Beach

- **Highlights**: A quieter, more relaxing alternative to Mamaia, with nearby cafes and restaurants.
- **Cost**: Free entry; affordable dining options nearby.
- **Why Visit?** Close to Constanța’s Old Town, making it great for a post-sightseeing break.
- [Discover more](rb.gy/5elcy1).

### 3. Vama Veche3. Vama Veche

- **Highlights**: Known for its bohemian vibe and laid-back atmosphere.
- **Cost**: Free; camping options are available.
- **Why Visit?** Perfect for backpackers and budget travelers seeking a unique beach experience.

## Best Time to Visit Constanța

- **Spring (April–June)**: Mild weather and fewer crowds. Ideal for sightseeing and outdoor activities.
- **Summer (July–August)**: Peak beach season with lively nightlife.
- **Fall (September–October)**: Warm weather lingers, and beaches are less crowded.

## Top Tourist Spots in Constanța

### 1. Constanța Casino1. Constanța Casino

- **Why Visit?** A symbol of the city, this Art Nouveau landmark offers stunning views of the Black Sea.
- **Cost**: Free.
- **Tips**: Visit early morning for fewer crowds and better photo opportunities.

### 2. Old Town Constanța2. Old Town Constanța

- **Why Visit?** Stroll through cobblestone streets lined with historic buildings and cafes.
- **Cost**: Free; guided tours cost $10–$20.
- [Learn more here](/2024/10/budget-travel-guide-to-romania-explore.html).

### 3. Dolphinarium3. Dolphinarium

- **Why Visit?** A fun activity for families, showcasing dolphin and seal shows.
- **Cost**: $10 for adults, $5 for children.

## Day-by-Day 7-Day Itinerary

### **Day 1: Arrival in Constanța**

- **Stay**: Hotel Megalos ($40/night).
- **Activities**: Explore Old Town and Constanța Casino.
- **Dinner**: Local seafood at Restaurant Reyna.
- **Total Cost**: $80.

### **Day 2: Mamaia Beach**

- **Morning**: Relax at Mamaia Beach.
- **Afternoon**: Try jet skiing or windsurfing.
- **Evening**: Enjoy vibrant nightlife at Fratelli Beach Club.
- **Total Cost**: $70.

### Day 3: Historical TourDay 3: Historical Tour

- **Visit**: The Archaeology Museum and Roman Mosaic Edifice.
- **Lunch**: Budget-friendly meal at Taverna Sarbului ($15).
- **Evening**: Sunset walk along the harbor.
- **Total Cost**: $60.

### **Day 4: Dolphinarium & Aqua Magic**

- **Morning**: Watch dolphin shows at the Dolphinarium.
- **Afternoon**: Enjoy slides and pools at Aqua Magic.
- **Total Cost**: $50.

### **Day 5: Vama Veche**

- **Travel**: Public bus ($5).
- **Stay**: Beachfront camping or local guesthouse ($20).
- **Activities**: Enjoy bonfires and live music.
- **Total Cost**: $40.

### Day 6: Local Markets & Food TourDay 6: Local Markets & Food Tour

- **Visit**: Ovidiu Square for souvenirs and street food.
- **Lunch**: Traditional Romanian dishes at Terasa Pescarus.
- **Total Cost**: $50.

### **Day 7: Departure**

- **Activities**: Final beach day at Modern Beach.
- **Total Cost**: $30.
