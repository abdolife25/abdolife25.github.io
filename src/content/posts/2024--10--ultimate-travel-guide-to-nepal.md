Nepal, a land of breathtaking landscapes, rich cultural heritage, and towering peaks, is a dream destination for travelers and adventurers alike. From the bustling streets of Kathmandu to the serene trails of the Himalayas, Nepal offers a unique blend of attractions and experiences. This guide will help you navigate your week in Nepal, detailing the best places to visit, hiking opportunities, travel packages, and a comprehensive budget.

## Best Time to Visit Nepal

The best time to visit Nepal largely depends on what you want to experience. Here’s a breakdown:

- **Spring (March to May)**: Ideal for trekking and exploring the natural beauty, with mild temperatures and blooming rhododendrons.
- **Autumn (September to November)**: Perfect for trekking with clear skies and stunning views of the Himalayas.
- **Winter (December to February)**: Good for cultural tours, but trekking can be challenging due to cold weather and snow.
- **Monsoon (June to August)**: Heavy rainfall can disrupt travel plans, making it less favorable for trekking.
