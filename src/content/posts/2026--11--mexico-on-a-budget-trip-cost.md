**Mexico costs roughly $45–75 a day on a tight budget and $80–155 a day at mid-range, flights not included.** It is cheapest in Mexico City and Oaxaca and between August and October, and most expensive in Cancún, Tulum and Los Cabos. The budget tricks are street tacos and market lunches, hostel dorms, first-class buses instead of domestic flights and a short list of paid sights.

## What Mexico costs per day (2027)

| | Tight budget | Mid-range |
|---|---|---|
| Bed | Hostel dorm $10–26 | Share a 3-star double $28–70 each |
| Food | $17–25 | $30–45 |
| Local transport | $10–14 | $13–23 |
| Sights | $7–13 | $14–29 |
| **Per day** | **about $45–75** | **about $80–155** |

Food is the best value. Street tacos in Mexico City cost about 15–20 pesos ($0.80–1.10) each and market meals about 80–120 pesos ($4.30–6.50). A *comida corrida* (a set lunch) is about $5–15, and in Oaxaca five tacos cost about $5, a tamal under $2.50 and a torta about $3. A mezcal pour at a bar is about $8 and a craft beer about $5. The Mexico City metro is 5 pesos ($0.27) a ride, one of the cheapest in the world.

Dorm prices vary. One guide lists Mexico City and Oaxaca dorms at about $10 a night and Cancún and Tulum from about $9, while another puts a dorm bed in central Oaxaca at about $26, and Oaxaca prices jump sharply around Day of the Dead, so treat these as planning ranges. A mid-range hotel room is about $56–63 a night in Mexico City and up to about $141 for a boutique hotel in Oaxaca.

## What 7 days costs in Mexico

Seven days and six nights on a Mexico City and Oaxaca route: three nights in Mexico City (with a day trip to Teotihuacan), a first-class bus, and three nights in Oaxaca (with Monte Albán).

| Cost (6 nights, 7 days) | Tight budget | Mid-range |
|---|---|---|
| Beds | $60–156 | $170–425 |
| Food | $120–175 | $210–315 |
| Bus and local transport | $70–95 | $90–160 |
| Sights and tours | $50–90 | $100–200 |
| **Total** | **about $300–515** | **about $570–1,100** |

The low end assumes $10 dorm beds, street food and market lunches, and a bus rather than a flight between the two cities. Mid-range assumes a shared double (the beds line is per person) and includes a full-day Oaxaca tour. As a check, one backpacker guide puts a social, hostel-based week at about $489 and another guide puts a mid-range day at about $90–150, both inside these ranges. Add 10–15% for extras.

### The route, day by day

#### Days 1–3: Mexico City

Fly into Mexico City. In the centre you can walk or ride the metro for about 5 pesos. Spend two days on street food, markets and museums, and one day at Teotihuacán, where foreign visitors pay 210 pesos ($11.40) at the gate. Eat at market stalls and ask for the *comida corrida* at lunch.

#### Day 4: bus to Oaxaca

Take the first-class ADO bus (about 6 hours). One 2026 guide puts the fare as low as about $35, and prices vary by time and class, so compare operators, book ahead and check the current fare on the ADO site. It is the cheapest way to cross the country, and flying costs more once you add airport transfers.

#### Days 4–6: Oaxaca

Oaxaca's food is the draw: tlayudas, tamales, tacos and mezcal at prices well below the capital's restaurants. Monte Albán costs 94 pesos ($5.10) to enter, plus a round-trip shuttle of about $7. A full-day tour that adds Hierve el Agua and a mezcal stop is about $72, plus about $12–18 for entry fees and food, so go on your own if you want to save. Book early if you visit around Day of the Dead, when beds are scarce and expensive.

![The gilded baroque ceiling of the Santo Domingo church in Oaxaca](/images/mexico-oaxaca-santo-domingo.jpg)
*Photo: [Jhovani Morales](https://www.pexels.com/photo/12319997/) on [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com)*

#### Day 7: home from Oaxaca

Fly home from Oaxaca (usually through Mexico City) or take the bus back. A colectivo from the Oaxaca airport to the centre is about $8 and a private taxi about $32, so share a ride into town when you arrive.

## Going to Cancún and the Riviera Maya instead

Cancún, Tulum and Playa del Carmen cost more than Mexico City and Oaxaca, and they add fees. Quintana Roo's Visitax is about 300 pesos ($16) for visitors to Cancún, Tulum and Playa del Carmen, and the Cancún hotel environmental tax is about 76 pesos ($4) per room per night. Entry to the ruins costs more too: Tulum is 210 pesos ($11.40) since the January 2026 increase, Uxmal 586 pesos ($31.70) and Chichén Itzá 676 pesos ($36.60) for foreign visitors. Mexico's federal visitor fee is about 983 pesos ($53) and is usually included in your airfare, so check your ticket. Plan on the mid-range column for the Riviera Maya, even with dorm beds.

## Cheapest time to go

August, September and October are the cheapest months for beds, with dorm prices around $12 a night, while February, March and July are the most expensive. This is also the rainy season in much of Mexico, and the Caribbean coast has hurricane risk from June to November, so check the forecast and your insurance. May has been cited as the cheapest month for flights from the US, with January, February and September also good value. Oaxaca's beds spike around Day of the Dead.

## Planning Mexico from your country

For most visitors the flight is the biggest single cost, so check fares from your own airport and compare more than one airline. Entry rules depend on your passport, so check the official Mexican immigration page before you book. Use bank ATMs rather than airport exchange kiosks, which can sit 8–15% below the mid-market rate. ## Frequently asked questions

**How much money do I need for 7 days in Mexico?** About $300–515 for a tight budget with dorm beds and $570–1,100 at mid-range, excluding flights, based on the Mexico City and Oaxaca route above.

**Is Mexico cheap?** Yes in Mexico City, Oaxaca and most of the interior, especially street food and buses. Cancún, Tulum, Los Cabos and the beach resorts are where costs climb.

**How much is the bus from Mexico City to Oaxaca?** As low as about $35 on a first-class ADO bus, taking about 6 hours. Confirm the current fare on the ADO site.

**How much does Chichén Itzá cost?** 676 pesos ($36.60) for foreign visitors in 2026, which is the highest of the Yucatán sites. Tulum and Teotihuacán are 210 pesos ($11.40) each.

**What are the extra fees in Cancún?** Quintana Roo's Visitax is about 300 pesos ($16), and the Cancún hotel environmental tax is about 76 pesos ($4) per room per night. The federal visitor fee of about 983 pesos ($53) is usually included in airfares.
